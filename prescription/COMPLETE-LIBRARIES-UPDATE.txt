SHARED DEMOS
------------
Demo rows use doctor_id = 0 and are visible to all approved doctors.
A doctor can remove a demo from their own account. This creates a record in
prescription_library_hidden and does not affect another doctor.

PERSONAL ITEMS
--------------
Items created by a doctor use that doctor's doctor_id. They are private and can
be edited, activated, deactivated or permanently deleted by that doctor only.

EDITOR
